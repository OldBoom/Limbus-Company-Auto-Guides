## Support Passive

### A Noble Duel
If the Speed of all allies is faster than the Speed of all enemies, this unit deals more damage based on the Speed difference between itself and the slowest enemy (+6% for every point of Speed difference; max 30%)

---

### Dueling Request
If the Speed of all allies is faster than the Speed of all enemies, the fastest ally deals more damage based on the Speed difference between itself and the slowest enemy (+3% for every point of Speed difference; max 15%)
