## Combat Passives

### I.L.T.L.T. (I would've Liked to walk The Lord's path Together)
When another ally's Skill hits an enemy: After that Attack, use "Hey, Back Off" as an Unopposed Attack against the enemy (once per turn) ; ; In the same turn this unit Substituted in or Returned to the battlefield, gain 2 Protector's Stance ; When this unit activates Unopposed Attack due to The Lord of Hongyuan Hong Lu's "The Heishou Lord" Passive, treat this unit as if it has "Heishou Pack" or "Jia Family" Trait Keywords

---

### Loyal Warrior

**(×3 Owned)**

If an ally is killed by enemy attack, or if the attacked ally's HP is at less than 25% after the attack: After that Attack, Drifting Blade of Hongyuan Ryōshū uses "Aiding Strike" as an Unopposed Attack against that enemy (once per Encounter) ; - The Lord of Hongyuan Hong Lu may activate this effect 1 more time past its usage limit ; ; If "Hey, Back Off" is activated via the Passive 'I.L.T.L.T.', the Skill deals +30% damage on Critical Hit and heals 2~4 SP for 1 other ally with the lowest SP (once per turn; excluding allies at -40 or lower SP)

### Drifter

**(×3)**

Combat Start: The ally with the earliest Deployment order gains 1 Shield for every Poise on self (max 10)
